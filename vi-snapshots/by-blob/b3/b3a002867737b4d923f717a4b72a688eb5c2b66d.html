Settings Editor.lvlib:Synchronize Module Events.vi

Ensure the module and its caller have appropriately created and registered for events. This VI should be called after a module's caller registers for events, but before the module's caller's event structure starts.

Wait on Event Sync must fire after Register for Events to ensure that no events are missed that are fired by the module after sync occurs.

Once Wait on Module Sync and Wait on Event Sync have both run, we know the broadcast events have been properly created by the module, and registered for by the tester. So it's safe to close down the two Rendezvous references...they are no longer needed.
